Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy explains what data the clipper desktop application ("the app") accesses, how it is used and how you can remove it. The app is a personal tool used only by its owner on their own computer.

Data the app accesses

  • Google / YouTube (scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly): the name and ID of the channel you choose when signing in, the ability to upload videos to that channel, and statistics (views, likes, comments, regional restrictions) of videos the app uploaded.
  • TikTok (scopes user.info.basic, video.upload, video.list): your account's open ID and display name, the ability to send videos to your TikTok account as drafts, and the list and view counts of your recent videos.

How the data is used

The data is used only to provide the app's features to you: to upload the videos you created to your own accounts, to show which account a video went to, and to display view statistics inside the app.

Storage

Access tokens and statistics are stored only on your own computer, in the app's folder (files tokens.json, settings.json and ledger.db). The app has no servers and does not send this data anywhere except directly to Google and TikTok when you use their APIs.

Sharing

The app does not sell, rent, share or transfer your data to any third party, does not use it for advertising and does not use it to train AI or machine-learning models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also uses YouTube API Services; by using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

  • Google: open myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove access for "clipper".
  • TikTok: in the TikTok app go to Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions and remove "clipper".
  • Locally: press "Disconnect" for the account in the app, or delete the files tokens.json and ledger.db from the app's folder.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly process their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
